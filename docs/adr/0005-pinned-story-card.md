# 5. The story card uses real sample numbers and unpins on mobile

Status: accepted · 2026-10-08

The start page's story card shows three steps (enter inputs → review cash flow → check assumptions). It uses the existing fictional sample property, and its figures come from the existing finance code, not hard-coded illustrations.

Desktop: the card is sticky and changes step as the reader scrolls past three text panels. On narrow screens (≤ 700px) it does not pin. The steps render as three stacked cards with the normal reveal.

Revised 2026-10-09: the story replaces both the "Review income, costs, and returns" sample-card section and the "Three steps" section (owner's choice). Their content moved into the three steps, and "View sample report" stays on the card. The anchor `#how-it-works` points at the story.
