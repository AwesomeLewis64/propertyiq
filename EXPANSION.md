# Monthly planner

Use the Monthly planner for lease events, renovations, construction, financing changes, owner cash calls, actuals, and source documentation. Use Quick analysis for annual stabilized underwriting and sensitivity.

Each project and section has a URL. Projects and backups remain browser-local. A URL alone does not transfer a private project to another browser; export a backup to transfer it.

The shared sample uses annual growth steps to reconcile with Quick analysis. Other projects can use continuous monthly growth. Dated monthly XIRR differs from annual IRR because distributions occur earlier.

See METHODOLOGY.md, TESTING.md and CHANGELOG.md.
